## Invoice filtering guide: implemented

Preserve title/H1 and working internal links. Add a compact table with three fictional records: pending, approved, and unreadable date. Explain why an approved document is not necessarily paid and why uncertain data must not silently disappear from the selected month. Explicitly distinguish reading records through an authorized TalkToData connection from OCR. Finish with a direct link to the automation service. Do not add vendor integration promises, accuracy percentages, real invoices, client names or new prices.

Acceptance: server-rendered semantic table with caption/headers; no horizontal overflow at 320px; readable light/dark; article FAQ and schema stay aligned; only this article's actual modified date changes.
